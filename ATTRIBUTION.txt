Mathematics: Introductory Statistics collection for Field Atlas (`openstax-introductory-statistics`).

This collection is built from the book:

Introductory Statistics 2e
Senior Contributing Authors: Barbara Illowsky, Susan Dean
OpenStax, Rice University
https://openstax.org/details/books/introductory-statistics-2e

The text is OpenStax's own, kept whole, one passage per numbered book
section (plus Mathematical Phrases, Symbols, and Formulas and Tables),
each followed by its glossary. Changes made: formulas are carried as LaTeX
converted from the book's MathML, tables are written one row to a line, the
circled letters that label the parts of a question are written "(a)", "(b)",
and images are left out (their captions and descriptions are kept).
The preface and the chapter introductions are not included.
No OpenStax logo is used, and OpenStax does not endorse Field Atlas.
